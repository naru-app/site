Naru (the “Service”) is an app for couples to see each other's day at a glance. This policy explains what the Service collects and how it is used, kept, and destroyed.

1. What We Collect

You sign in with an Apple or Google account. The Service does not send its own verification emails.

  • Email address — received from Apple or Google at sign-in and used to identify your account. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive an Apple-generated relay address instead, and the Service never learns your real address.
  • Nickname — the name shown to your partner
  • Your main city (approximate location and time zone) — used to show both people's local times and weather. Location may be read once while you pick a city; it is never tracked continuously.
  • Sleep hours (optional) — the approximate bedtime and wake time you enter, used to show whether your partner is likely asleep and to work out when you are both free
  • Naru character — the colour and prop you choose. The Service does not collect profile photos.
  • Events, notes, bucket items, and your anniversary — what you enter in the app. Notes may contain drawings or attached images; bucket items may contain links and preview images. Your anniversary is the date you started dating, used to show how many days you've been together.
  • Moments — photos you take with the camera in the Moment tab, along with when they were taken and the city and weather at the time. We keep only the most recent photo for each person and delete the previous one when you take a new one. They are also deleted when you disconnect from your partner or delete your account.
  • Events from connected calendars (optional) — if you connect iPhone Calendar or Google Calendar, the titles, times, and notes (descriptions) of events in the calendars you select are brought to our servers and shown to your partner. For Google Calendar we store your Google account email and an encrypted refresh token to keep the connection alive, using read-only access. You can disconnect at any time in the app; doing so deletes the imported events and the token.
  • Device token for notifications — if you turn notifications on, the device token issued by Apple. Notification text includes minimal content such as your partner's nickname and a note preview, and passes through Apple's push servers on the way to your device.
  • Purchase records — if you buy a paid membership, the transaction identifier, product, and purchase and expiry times we receive from Apple. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Apple and never reach the Service.
  • Usage records — last active time, how far you got in the app's screens, whether you have installed and used widgets, and app version. These are kept to run and improve the Service, not for advertising or behavioural tracking.
  • Consent history — when you agreed, and which versions of the Terms and this policy you agreed to
  • Feedback and reports (optional) — if you send feedback or a report, its content, the reply email you enter (if any), app version, device, and language, plus the reported content and the outcome

Clipboard — on the invite-code screen, the Service only checks whether your clipboard holds a number or a link, to decide whether to show a paste button. It reads the clipboard only when you tap paste, and stores or sends nothing from it other than the invite code you paste.

Neither the app nor the website uses cookies or similar tools that automatically collect behavioural information.

2. Why We Use It

We use this information only to provide and improve the Service — showing both people's local times, weather, events, notes, bucket items, and Moments; working out when you are both free; sending notifications; confirming payments and granting membership access; managing accounts; usage statistics and improvements; handling feedback; handling reports and responding to violations of our Terms; and beta invitations and announcements. We do not advertise, track behaviour, or sell data, and we use no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Sharing With Your Partner

Once you connect with a partner, they can see your nickname, city, time zone, sleep hours, Naru character, events, notes, shared bucket items, Moments, and anniversary. This is the core of the Service.

Events from connected calendars are also shown to your partner. If you connect iPhone or Google Calendar, the titles, times, and notes of events in the calendars you select are visible to them. You choose which calendars to share in the app; calendars you don't select are never imported.

When you disconnect, sharing stops and imported calendar events and Moments are deleted. Anything already shown on your partner's device before then cannot be recalled.

4. When the Operator Reviews Content

The operator does not routinely look at your events, notes, photos, or other content. We may view specific content, only as far as needed, when:

  • a user reports it — the reported content and what is needed to judge the report
  • it is suspected of breaking the law or our Terms, such as non-consensual intimate images or child sexual abuse material
  • law enforcement or another authority requests it through lawful process
  • you ask us to check something to resolve your inquiry

Any action we take as a result (removing content, restricting use, reporting to authorities) follows our Terms of Service.

5. Storage and International Transfers

Data is stored with the cloud backend Supabase, on servers located in Singapore. Your personal data is therefore stored and processed outside Korea to provide the Service (to perform our agreement with you).

  • Supabase, Inc. (Singapore region) — what: everything listed in “1. What We Collect” · why: storing and processing Service data · how long: same as “6. How Long We Keep It” · contact: supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple Inc. (Apple's servers, including in the US) — if notifications are on, notification text and the device token (APNs); your city's coordinates to show the weather (WeatherKit); transaction information to confirm purchases · why: delivering notifications, weather, and purchase confirmation · how long: per Apple's policy · contact: apple.com/legal/privacy/contact
  • Google LLC (Google's servers, including in the US) — if you sign in with Google or connect Google Calendar, what is needed for sign-in and reading events · why: sign-in and calendar sync · how long: per Google's policy · contact: Google privacy contact

When and how — data is sent over the network whenever you use the app, and traffic between your device and servers is encrypted with HTTPS.

How to refuse, and what happens — the transfer to Supabase is essential to the Service, so if you don't want it you can stop using the Service by deleting your account in the app; you will then be unable to use the Service. Notifications and Google Calendar are optional: turning them off or disconnecting stops those transfers, and everything else keeps working. Weather lookups use only your city's location, never your account information.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your personal data for as long as your account exists and destroy it without delay when you delete the account. Feedback you sent is deleted along with the account. Beta applications previously submitted through the website are kept for 90 days from submission and then destroyed; you can also ask us to delete them sooner using the contact below.

We back up the database daily for disaster recovery and keep each backup for up to 30 days before it is deleted in turn. Data you delete disappears from the Service right away but may remain in backups for up to 30 days, during which it is used only for disaster recovery.

The following remain for a set period even after account deletion.

  • Payment records — transaction identifiers and payment or refund times. Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce requires records of payment and supply to be kept for 5 years, so we keep them for that period and then destroy them. No identifying details such as your name or email are kept with them.
  • Report and enforcement records — the report, the reported content, and the outcome. To prevent repeat harm and handle disputes, we keep them for 1 year after the case is closed and then destroy them. If we reported a violation to the authorities, we may keep them as long as that process requires.
  • Deletion record — for statistics that help us improve, we keep only when the account was deleted and how far the person got after signing up. It contains no nickname, email, event, or other content.

Where other laws require retention, we may keep data for the period they specify.

7. How We Destroy Data

Personal data whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been fulfilled is destroyed without delay. Data we must keep by law is stored separately from other data. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered; the Service does not keep personal data on paper.

8. Deleting Your Account and Data

  • Use “Delete Account” in the app to permanently delete your account and the data you added (profile, events, notes, Moments, and bucket items you saved) at any time.
  • Deleting your account unpairs you; anything your partner added stays with them.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Backups, payment records required by law, report and enforcement records, and deletion statistics containing no content remain as described in “6. How Long We Keep It”.

9. How We Protect It

  • Access control — row-level database policies let only you and your connected partner read your shared data, and let you write only your own. Photos are kept in private storage with the same restrictions.
  • Encryption — all traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and Google Calendar refresh tokens are stored encrypted with a separate key.
  • Minimal admin access — the operator's admin screen shows aggregate statistics, recent sign-ups' nicknames and last active times, and the feedback you send us; it does not show events, notes, photos, or other content. Admin rights are limited to as few people as possible, and the backup account can only read.
  • Collecting less — we do not collect payment details, contacts, profile photos, or continuous location.

10. Children Under 14

The Service is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Third Parties and Processors

Except where required by law, we do not sell or hand personal data to third parties. We entrust processing only to the infrastructure provider needed to run the Service (Supabase). Sign-in happens through Apple or Google accounts, and we receive the information needed — such as an email address — from whichever provider you choose.

Where the law requires or permits reporting — for example non-consensual intimate images or child sexual abuse material — or where an authority requests it through lawful process, we may provide the necessary information to the relevant authorities.

If you connect Google Calendar, the Service receives calendar information from Google within the read-only scope you granted. Information received from Google is used only for the purposes listed in “2. Why We Use It”; it is not used for advertising, not sold to third parties, and not read by humans. You can disconnect at any time in the app or from your Google account security settings.

Naru's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments for paid memberships are processed by Apple. The Service receives no payment method details — only the transaction identifier and status needed to confirm a purchase.

12. Your Rights

You may ask at any time to see, correct, or delete your personal data, or to stop its processing. In the app you can view and change your nickname, city, sleep hours, character, anniversary, and notification settings; you can disconnect a connected calendar at any time; and you can delete your data by deleting your account. For anything else, please use the contact below — we will handle it without delay and let you know the result.
