Privacy Policy

Service: Oegyeeo (외계어) · Effective: October 20, 2026

In short — If you use the app without signing in, we collect no personal data and your learning record stays on your device. If you sign in with Apple, a backup of your learning record and a daily activity record are stored on a server in Seoul, South Korea, and deleting your account in the app deletes them immediately. The English sentences you write are graded on your device only. No ads, no tracking.

Oegyeeo (외계어, the “Service”) is a business-English learning app for people who work at global companies. This policy explains what the Service collects and how it is used.

1. Using the app without signing in

  • Signing in is optional. Every learning feature works without an account, and in that case the Service collects no personal data.
  • Your learning record (expressions you have studied and their review schedule, recall and decoding history, saved sentences, the English sentences you wrote and their results, and the days you studied) is stored only on this device and is not sent to any server.
  • Writing is graded on your device. Reviewed rules grade first, and when Apple Intelligence is available on the device, Apple Foundation Models running on the device assist. Your sentences are never sent to a server or an outside AI service for grading.
  • Questions and reports by email — if you send a question or report by email, including through “이상한 점 알리기” (Report a problem) in the app, we receive that email (your email address and what it contains — the card name and version, the app version, the answer you wrote and its result, and anything you add) and use it only to reply and to fix our content. You send the email yourself from your mail app; the app sends nothing on its own.

2. What we collect if you sign in with Apple

If you want to back up your learning record, you can sign in with Apple. The Service does not request your name or email address from Apple. When you sign in, we store the following on our server:

  • Account identifier — your Service account ID and the user identifier Apple issues to this app
  • Learning record backup — the entire learning record described in section 1, including the English sentences you wrote. With each backup we store the app version and the device model (e.g. “iPhone”); we do not send the name you gave your device.
  • Daily activity record — the date you opened the app while signed in (one row per day) and the time it was first recorded that day
  • Consent record — when you agreed, and which versions of the Terms and this Policy

As part of running the server, our infrastructure provider may keep access logs, such as request times and IP addresses, for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use it

  • To identify your account and keep you signed in
  • To back up your learning record and restore it on a new device or after reinstalling
  • For usage statistics, such as daily active users, and to improve the Service (daily activity record)
  • To confirm your consent to the Terms
  • To answer questions and reports and to fix our content (emails you send us)

We do not show ads, track you, or sell data, and we do not use any advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

4. Where it is stored, and security

Data of signed-in users is stored with Supabase, a cloud backend service, on servers in Seoul, South Korea (ap-northeast-2). The Service does not move personal data to be stored outside South Korea.

Traffic between your device and the server is encrypted with HTTPS, and access rules ensure each user can read and write only the data of their own account.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we immediately delete the account, the learning record backup, the daily activity record, and the consent record from the server.

After deletion we keep only the time of deletion, for statistics on account deletions. It contains no account identifier, learning record, or anything else that could identify you. We may keep data for longer only where the law requires it.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app, go to Record tab (기록) → Account (계정) → “Delete account” (계정 삭제) to delete your account and the record backed up on the server at any time. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Deleting your account keeps the learning record on this device. The record on the device is removed when you delete the app.
  • Signing out does not delete data on the server.
  • You can disconnect Sign in with Apple yourself under “Sign in with Apple” in the Apple Account section of your iPhone’s Settings.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Sharing and processors

Except as required by law, we do not sell or provide personal data to third parties. We use the following processor only as needed to run the Service:

  • Supabase Inc. — storage and processing of accounts, learning record backups, and daily activity records (servers in Seoul)

Sign-in happens through your Apple account, and in that process we receive a user identifier from Apple.

9. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal data. You can sign out and delete your account in the app; for anything else, contact us below.

10. Privacy officer

  • Privacy officer: Deborah Lee
  • Contact: slee.iconlab@gmail.com

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and announce the effective date at least 7 days in advance. Significant changes that are adverse to users, such as collecting more data or using it for new purposes, will be announced at least 30 days in advance, and we will ask for your consent again where required.
